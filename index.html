Behind The Formulation

Formulation Without Compromise

Aura Botanica was born from a fundamental belief: that therapeutic plant chemistry can outperform conventional synthetic skincare when sourced sustainably and extracted cold.

Every bottle originates in our dedicated botanical laboratory, using heirloom Damascus roses, hand-harvested green tea leaves, and first-press cold-pressed rosehip seed oil. We never heat our ingredients above ambient room temperature, ensuring zero loss of delicate phytonutrients, polyphenols, and essential fatty acids.
